Important field limitation:
The DataHub squads.csv schema supplies team, player, position and shirt-number fields. It does not supply the club a player represented at the time of the tournament. FOOTBALL ARCADE stores historical club as null rather than inventing or substituting a current club. A separate, independently sourced and licensed historical club dataset is required before club hints can be enabled.
