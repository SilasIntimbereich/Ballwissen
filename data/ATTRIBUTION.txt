Important field limitation:
The DataHub squads.csv schema supplies team, player, position and shirt-number fields. It does not supply the club a player represented at the time of the tournament. FOOTBALL ARCADE therefore keeps unverified historical club fields null rather than inventing or substituting a current club. Club hints are enabled only for teams with complete independent player-level audit records. The current ledger has 44 verified historical teams; 148 historical teams remain open.

Official 2026 cross-check:
FIFA final squad lists:
https://fdp.fifa.org/assetspublic/ce281/pdf/SquadLists-English.pdf
FIFA announcement confirming 48 teams and 1,248 players:
https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/media-releases/world-cup-2026-48-squads-confirmed

The 2026 snapshot contains 1,248 rows across 48 teams, 26 per team. All 48 teams have complete player-level comparisons against FIFA's final squad PDF and the independent OpenFootball-derived snapshot, including shirt number, player identity, position and tournament-time club. The audit ledger records 1,248 player rows across 48 verified teams. The NBC Sports June 11 list is retained as a dated tertiary comparison; its differences are reported separately because injury replacements and classification changes occurred after that snapshot. The automated checker does not silently overwrite FIFA's final list.
