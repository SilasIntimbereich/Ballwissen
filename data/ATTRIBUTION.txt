Important field limitation:
The DataHub squads.csv schema supplies team, player, position and shirt-number fields. It does not supply the club a player represented at the time of the tournament. FOOTBALL ARCADE stores historical club as null rather than inventing or substituting a current club. A separate, independently sourced and licensed historical club dataset is required before historical club hints can be enabled.

Official 2026 cross-check:
FIFA final squad lists:
https://fdp.fifa.org/assetspublic/ce281/pdf/SquadLists-English.pdf
FIFA announcement confirming 48 teams and 1,248 players:
https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/media-releases/world-cup-2026-48-squads-confirmed

The 2026 snapshot contains 1,248 rows across 48 teams, 26 per team. Twenty teams have a complete player-level comparison against FIFA's final PDF and an independent secondary roster source; the other 28 remain unverified. The automated PDF.js checker reports discrepancies but does not automatically approve a team.
